PhD at the University of Minnesota. Actively looking for full-time opportunities.

I work on **spatial data science, GeoAI, and trustworthy spatiotemporal machine learning**, with a focus on physics-informed and knowledge-guided methods for AI4Science. Most of what I do is hands-on: real code, reproducible pipelines, and papers with running artifacts behind them.

My dissertation, *Distortion-Aware Spatial Data Science* (University of Minnesota, defended 2025), builds detectors for deception and denial in movement data: abnormal trajectory-gap detection, GPS-spoofing and rendezvous detection over maritime AIS, and space-time-prism reasoning for what a moving object could plausibly have done.

### What I work on

- **Spatial & spatiotemporal ML:** trajectory anomaly detection, maritime AIS analytics, space-time prisms, distortion-aware geospatial correction
- **Physics-informed & knowledge-guided ML:** diffusion models for trajectory generation, Kriging-informed downscaling, surrogate spatial neural networks
- **AI4Science:** climate and Earth-system emulation, soil and greenhouse-gas modeling, spatial domain adaptation for oncology imaging
- **Agentic & applied AI:** spatiotemporal reasoning agents, physics-informed RL reward stacks, production-grade ML services
- **Reproductions that run:** independent, tested re-implementations of research methods (Rust, CUDA, PyTorch)
